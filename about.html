Our story

It started with a rash that wouldn't go away.

Verdora wasn't planned as a company. It was one woman, a bad reaction to a "clean" drugstore serum, and a windowsill of calendula.

Amina's kitchen counter, 2021.

Amina Zafar had spent a decade in food science, testing preservation methods for a packaged goods company. When her skin started reacting to nearly everything on the market, she went back to what she knew: small batches, real ingredients, and knowing exactly what went into a jar.

She started growing calendula and chamomile in raised beds behind her apartment, pressing her own oils, and testing balms on herself first — always herself first. Two years and a hundred failed batches later, Verdora's first Repair Balm went to twelve friends. All twelve asked for more.

What we believe

Three rules we don't bend on.

01

Know the grower

Every botanical is traceable to a farm and a name. If we can't visit it, we don't source from it.

02

Formulate for sensitivity

Every product is patch-tested on genuinely reactive skin before it's tested on anyone else's.

03

Batch small, waste less

We make what we can sell in six weeks. No warehouses of stock quietly expiring.

How we got here

A slow build, on purpose.

2021

First calendula bed planted behind a rented apartment in Abbottabad.

2022

Repair Balm sold to the public for the first time, at a local farmers' market.

2024

Partnered with 12 small growers; moved to refillable glass packaging.

2026

Verdora ships nationwide from a single small studio, still batch-tested by hand.
