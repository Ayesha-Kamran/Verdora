Our impact

The numbers behind the claims.

"Sustainable" gets used loosely in this industry. Here's exactly what we measure, and where we still fall short.

Sourcing

100% of our botanicals come from farms we've personally visited. 12 of 12 partner growers are certified organic or funded through a transition plan we co-finance.

Packaging

92% of units ship in refillable glass or compostable kraft. The remaining 8% — pump mechanisms — are the hardest part to solve, and we say so rather than hide it.

Waste

We batch to demand, not forecast. Unsold stock older than 90 days is offered at cost to local skin clinics rather than discarded.

Where we're still working

Honest gaps, not a highlight reel.

Shipping emissions

Cold-weather orders currently ship with standard carriers. We're piloting a carbon-offset shipping option in late 2026.

Pump packaging

Our two pump-dispensed products still use mixed-material bottles. We haven't found a refillable pump we trust yet — we're still looking.
